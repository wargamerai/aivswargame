Wooden Ships & Iron Men: Battle Reports

Action reports sent in by players. Each report shows the map with every ship's track and broadside, the course of the battle and the final result.

Reports you send us will be published on this page. If you do not want your report published, please say so in your email.

How to send a report
  1. During or after a battle, press "📜 Report" in the bar at the bottom of the battle screen (next to "💾 Save"). When a battle ends, the report is also saved automatically.
  2. Your browser saves a file named like wsim_report_Sc10_T9_….html to your Downloads folder.
  3. Attach the file to an email to gbgty203@yahoo.co.jp. If you found a bug, tell us briefly what went wrong.
